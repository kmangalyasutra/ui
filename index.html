Need to know

Questions, answered

Is registration free?

Yes. mangalyasutra.com is 100% free, with no membership fees.

How do I register?

Fill in the short form on this page, enter the code we send to your mobile, then complete your profile.

Do I have to fill in everything?

No. There are only six quick fields. You can add more to your profile after you register.

Why do you ask for my age?

To make sure every member is of legal marriageable age under Indian law (21 for men, 18 for women).

How does mobile verification work?

We send a one-time code to your mobile number. You log in with a code each time, so there is no password to remember.

What can other members see about me?

Only logged-in members of the opposite gender can see your profile. Your name is shortened in search results, and your mobile number is never shown.

Can I hide my profile?

Yes. Switch off "Show my profile in search" in My profile at any time.

What is an interest?

It is your way of saying hello. When the other person accepts, you can message each other.

How do I delete my account?

Contact us from the Contact page and we will remove your account and data.
